## Description

Full technical leadership in the development of an iOS project (Alugue.com), from planning through publication on the App Store. The focus was on establishing a robust, scalable, and maintainable codebase.

## What I worked with

The project was structured using **DDD (Domain-Driven Design)** and **SOLID** principles, with **Clean Architecture** and **MVP (Model-View-Presenter)**. I ensured code quality through **TDD (Test-Driven Development)** and **unit tests** (XCTest). Dependencies were efficiently managed with **Swift Package Manager (SPM)**, and I was responsible for the complete management of Certificates and Profiles.
