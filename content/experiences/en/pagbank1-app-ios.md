## Description

Development and maintenance of essential features in a Core Banking project, focused on stability, quality, and code maintainability.

## What I worked with

I applied the **MVVM-C design pattern** to ensure a cleaner, maintainable, and testable software structure. I **integrated and consumed RESTful/JSON APIs**. The focus on **code quality** was reinforced through **unit tests** (XCTest) and **SwiftLint** to maintain consistent standards. I worked in agile teams, actively using **Kanban** and **Scrum** methodologies.
