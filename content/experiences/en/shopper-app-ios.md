## Description

Development of new features (including IOT integration), with a strong focus on improving delivery processes, automation, and architecture.

## What I worked with

I implemented **DevOps** practices by configuring and managing **CI/CD** pipelines with **Fastlane** and **Semaphore CI**, automating publishing and distribution processes. I **refactored** legacy code, improving the architecture to **MVVM-C**. I managed **Certificates and Profiles** through both manual processes and **automation** with **Fastlane Match**. The development workflow was standardized with **GitFlow**.
