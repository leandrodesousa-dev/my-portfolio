## Description

A project focused on maintenance, development of new features, and management of the complete App Store distribution lifecycle for the Communy application.

## What I worked with

I managed the **complete distribution lifecycle**, including **Certificates and Provisioning Profiles**, and the release of new versions on the **App Store** and **Firebase App Distribution**. I developed new features, such as the leisure area booking system, and performed **maintenance and refactoring** of legacy code to adhere to new technical agreements. Quality was ensured through **unit tests** with **XCTest**.
